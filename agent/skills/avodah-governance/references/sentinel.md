# 4. Sentinel-Led Restorative Accountability Framework

Internal covenant alignment and operational oversights are governed by the Sentinel-led restorative accountability framework. This replaces adversarial review with structural remediation.

## Non-Punitive Audits

Deviations in protocol, missed deadlines, or ledger discrepancies trigger an automatic restorative inquiry facilitated by the Sentinel rather than formal administrative sanctions.

## Three-Step Remediation Pathway

1. **Root-Cause Ingestion:** Automated capture of system logs and workflow telemetry to determine the source of friction.
2. **Covenantal Alignment Session:** Structured review between the Sentinel and the responsible steward to establish corrective measures.
3. **Restorative Reintegration:** Re-certification of task workflows and ledger ratification without adverse mark on permanent operational standing.

## Jurisdictional Scope Boundary

Internal Sentinel restorative procedures apply strictly to operational covenants and internal practice standards, preserving absolute separation from external statutory compliance obligations under Kansas law.

When a missed item is a statutory obligation (for example a FinCEN BOI filing or a Kansas annual/biennial report), the restorative pathway may address the internal process failure, but the external filing must still be cured through the governing authority on its own terms.
